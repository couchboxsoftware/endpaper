## 0.1.2 · 19 Nov 2026
A menu bar like other Windows apps, inside covers, and covers as single pages.

### New
- A menu bar along the top (File, Edit, View, Insert, Type, Object, Page, Help) with the shortcuts shown. Alt+F opens File, Alt on its own highlights the bar, and the arrow keys move around.
- Print the inside of the cover too (Pages, Cover, Print the inside too): the inside front cover and inside back cover, with the glue area down the spine marked.
- Covers can be single pages: the front and back (and the inside front and back) as separate pages, to work on and to export (Pages, Cover, Single pages). Switch back to one spread at any time and nothing is lost.
- Choose the order of single cover pages in the cover PDF (Properties, Layout, Page order).
- Full screen with F11, and Settings with Ctrl+, from anywhere.
- Check warns when something on the inside cover is in the glue area, and when something is only on the spine while the cover is single pages.

### Fixed
- Double-clicking a text box opens it for editing again, so you can change or add to text after leaving it.
- Photos on a cover that's switched off are kept when you save.

## 0.1.1 · 19 Nov 2026
The close button works, and Endpaper opens with a splash screen.

### New
- An open-book splash screen shows while Endpaper loads your fonts and printing profile. When everything is ready, the page turns and Endpaper opens.

### Fixed
- The close button (X) closes Endpaper. It still asks first if your design has unsaved changes.

## 0.1.0 · 16 Nov 2026
Endpaper makes print-ready designs and books in CMYK.

### New
- New design and New book start from paper, card, poster and book sizes, or any size you type in mm, cm or px.
- Text boxes, photo frames, rectangles, ellipses and lines can be dragged, resized and rotated, and snap to edges, margins and each other (hold Ctrl to stop snapping).
- Layers list everything on the page, top first: drag to reorder, and hide or lock anything.
- Bleed, trim and the safe area show on every page, and photos and backgrounds can run into the bleed.
- Colours are CMYK, shown as they'll print on the printing profile you choose (Settings, Colour).
- Swatches and text styles: change one and everything using it changes.
- Books have facing pages, masters with page numbers ({page}), and a cover with the spine worked out from the page count and paper.
- Text can continue from one box into another, and the Story window shows all of it.
- Photos show their resolution as you resize them, and turn yellow or red when they'd print soft.
- Check lists what a printer would flag: low-resolution photos, text that doesn't fit, things that stop short of the bleed, text outside the safe area, rich black small text and too much ink.
- Export makes PDF/X-4 files with CMYK photos, embedded fonts, bleed and crop marks, or PNG and JPG at any resolution.
- Book export makes the inside pages and the cover as two PDFs, with or without bleed on the spine edge.
- The brand kit keeps your colours, text styles and logos ready for every design.
- Save any design as a template, and start new work from it on the start screen. Deleted templates stay in the bin for 30 days.
- Unsaved work is kept safe every 20 seconds, and offered back if Endpaper closes unexpectedly.
- Choose how Endpaper opens: in a window, maximised, full screen, or as you left it (Settings, General).
