## 0.1.11 · 24 Nov 2026
Layer effects on your photos, like a photo editor.

### New
- Edit photo (Object, Edit photo, or Ctrl+Shift+E) opens a photo large, with its effects listed beside it like layers: add, hide, reorder by dragging, set the strength, and hold Before / After to compare.
- 24 effects: brightness and contrast, exposure, shadows and highlights, levels, curves, saturation and vibrance, warmth and tint, hue shift, black and white, duotone, colour overlay, invert, sharpen, blur, noise reduction, vignette, grain, halftone, threshold, posterise, flip and rotate, fade edges, blend modes and drop shadow.
- A photo's Properties lists its effects, to switch each one on or off without opening the window. Edit photo… is there and in the right-click menu too.
- Effects on a master's photo carry into a page's own copy (Ctrl+Shift+click), ready to change on that page. Effects you leave alone there still follow the master.
- Effects print as they look: they're in the PDF and PNG or JPG export, with fades, shadows and blend modes kept.

## 0.1.10 · 23 Nov 2026
Know what each copy weighs.

### New
- File, Shipment preparation sets the paper and binding (paperback or hardback; the weight, finish and shade of the pages and the cover) and shows the finished size and weight of one copy, part by part.
- New book has More options for the same paper and binding, so the spine is right from the start.
- Type in your printer's paper thickness (File, Shipment preparation) and it's used instead of the estimate.

### Improved
- The spine is worked out from the paper's weight and finish. The cover's Properties sums up the paper in one line, with Change… to open Shipment preparation.
- Books made before this keep exactly the spine they had.

## 0.1.9 · 23 Nov 2026
Hold your book before it's printed.

### New
- View, Preview book (Ctrl+Shift+P) shows a book with a cover as the real thing: open it, turn the pages, and turn it over to see the spine and back.
- Pages turn by clicking, dragging the edge or the arrow keys, and pages bend like paper while the covers stay stiff.

## 0.1.8 · 20 Nov 2026
Cover page thumbnails stay right when something crosses the fold.

### Fixed
- With the cover as single pages, changing something that crosses the fold updates the other side's thumbnail too, on the outside and the inside cover (Pages, Cover).
- Typing in the Story window updates the page thumbnails as you go.

## 0.1.7 · 20 Nov 2026
Open what you export straight away, in Inkdesktop if you have it.

### New
- Open file(s) after export, a tick box in the Export window that stays as you last left it.
- With Inkdesktop, our PDF app, installed, it opens your PDF in Inkdesktop. A book opens its inside pages, and the folder shows the cover beside them.
- Without Inkdesktop, it asks whether you'd like to try it before the PDF opens in your usual PDF viewer. Tick "Don't ask again" to stop it, and bring it back in Settings, Hints.
- Pictures exported as PNG or JPG open their folder, rather than a window for each one.

## 0.1.6 · 19 Nov 2026
Endpaper now looks and behaves like the rest of the Couchbox Software family.

### New
- After an update, a card in the bottom-left corner shows what's new in that version, once, with a button to see every version.
- What's new shows New, Improved and Fixed as coloured titles (green, blue and amber), with "You have this" on the version you're using (Settings, App).
- Endpaper is free to use and share under the MIT licence.

### Improved
- Endpaper checks for an update as soon as it opens, instead of waiting a few seconds.
- The endpaper name is drawn in the family style, with "paper" in magenta, on the start screen, the splash and in Settings.

- New installs open maximised. If you've already chosen how Endpaper opens, that stays as it is (Settings, General).
- The update banner always says whether your design will be saved first, even when it appears straight after Endpaper opens.
- Settings opens on the tab you used last, also with Ctrl+, (File, Settings).

### Fixed
- The start screen says where Save as template is (File, Save as template).

## 0.1.5 · 19 Nov 2026
Fill in a master's boxes page by page, and hints that explain how.

### New
- Hold Ctrl and Shift and click a box from the master to change it on that page only. Other pages keep the master's version, and anything you leave alone on the page (like where the box sits) still follows the master.
- Right-click a page's copy and choose Reset to master to go back to the master's version.
- Empty text boxes show a faint outline on screen, so placeholders on a master can be seen on its pages.
- Hint pop-ups explain things the first time you need them, starting with editing master boxes. Tick "Don't show this again" to stop one.
- Settings, Hints lists every hint, so you can show one again or all of them (also Help, Show all hints again).
- File, Settings opens each part of Settings directly, and View, Snap to edges and boxes switches snapping on and off.

### Improved
- The gear buttons are gone: everything they opened is in the menus.

## 0.1.4 · 19 Nov 2026
Every text box has its own settings.

### Improved
- Font, size, spacing, colour and alignment in Properties change only the text boxes you've selected. Other boxes, and the text style they started from, stay as they are.
- Select several text boxes to change them together. Settings they share show in Properties; ones that differ are left blank, and anything you set goes to all of them.
- Text styles are starting points: pick one in Properties (or click it in Colours) to give the selected text its settings, and Reset to style puts a box back.
- Editing a text style asks whether to update the boxes that use it.

### Fixed
- Vertical alignment (top, middle, bottom) no longer changes every box with the same text style.

## 0.1.3 · 19 Nov 2026
Double-click a text box to type in it again.

### Fixed
- Double-clicking a text box opens it so you can change or add to the text. Double-clicking a photo crops it, or picks a photo if the frame is empty.
- Clicking inside text you're typing moves the cursor there, instead of closing the text box.

## 0.1.2 · 19 Nov 2026
A menu bar like other Windows apps, inside covers, and covers as single pages.

### New
- A menu bar along the top (File, Edit, View, Insert, Type, Object, Page, Help) with the shortcuts shown. Alt+F opens File, Alt on its own highlights the bar, and the arrow keys move around.
- Print the inside of the cover too (Pages, Cover, Print the inside too): the inside front cover and inside back cover, with the glue area down the spine marked.
- Covers can be single pages: the front and back (and the inside front and back) as separate pages, to work on and to export (Pages, Cover, Single pages). Switch back to one spread at any time and nothing is lost.
- Choose the order of single cover pages in the cover PDF (Properties, Layout, Page order).
- Full screen with F11, and Settings with Ctrl+, from anywhere.
- Check warns when something on the inside cover is in the glue area, and when something is only on the spine while the cover is single pages.

### Fixed
- Double-clicking a text box opens it for editing again, so you can change or add to text after leaving it.
- Photos on a cover that's switched off are kept when you save.

## 0.1.0 · 16 Nov 2026
Endpaper makes print-ready designs and books in CMYK.

### New
- New design and New book start from paper, card, poster and book sizes, or any size you type in mm, cm or px.
- Text boxes, photo frames, rectangles, ellipses and lines can be dragged, resized and rotated, and snap to edges, margins and each other (hold Ctrl to stop snapping).
- Layers list everything on the page, top first: drag to reorder, and hide or lock anything.
- Bleed, trim and the safe area show on every page, and photos and backgrounds can run into the bleed.
- Colours are CMYK, shown as they'll print on the printing profile you choose (Settings, Colour).
- Swatches and text styles: change one and everything using it changes.
- Books have facing pages, masters with page numbers ({page}), and a cover with the spine worked out from the page count and paper.
- Text can continue from one box into another, and the Story window shows all of it.
- Photos show their resolution as you resize them, and turn yellow or red when they'd print soft.
- Check lists what a printer would flag: low-resolution photos, text that doesn't fit, things that stop short of the bleed, text outside the safe area, rich black small text and too much ink.
- Export makes PDF/X-4 files with CMYK photos, embedded fonts, bleed and crop marks, or PNG and JPG at any resolution.
- Book export makes the inside pages and the cover as two PDFs, with or without bleed on the spine edge.
- The brand kit keeps your colours, text styles and logos ready for every design.
- Save any design as a template, and start new work from it on the start screen. Deleted templates stay in the bin for 30 days.
- Unsaved work is kept safe every 20 seconds, and offered back if Endpaper closes unexpectedly.
- Choose how Endpaper opens: in a window, maximised, full screen, or as you left it (Settings, General).
