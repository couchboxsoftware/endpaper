## 0.1.20 · 25 Nov 2026
Group things to move and scale them together, and photos resize the way you'd expect.

### New
- Group things (Ctrl+G, or Object, Group) to keep them together, and Ungroup with Ctrl+Shift+G. One click picks up the whole group; double-click to work on one thing in it, and Esc to come back out.
- A group's corners scale everything in it together, text sizes and line widths too, and the handle on top turns it all.
- Layers shows each group as a folder: open or close it, hide or lock all of it, double-click to rename it, and drag things into or out of it.
- Select several things and their corners scale them together, without grouping.

### Improved
- A photo's corner scales the frame and photo together, so the same part stays in view. Hold Shift to change the frame's shape instead.
- A photo's side crops it: the photo stays where it is, and only grows if the frame would otherwise show an empty gap.
- Fill frame and Fit in frame are buttons in a photo's Properties that fit it once, so resizing always works the same way afterwards.
- Duplicating, pasting or Alt-dragging a group makes a new group of its own.

## 0.1.19 · 25 Nov 2026
Remove a photo's background in one click, then touch it up.

### New
- Remove background, a new photo effect (Edit photo, Add, Shape and blending), finds the subject (people, pets, products) and removes the rest. It works on your own computer: nothing is uploaded.
- Touch up the edges on the photo: the wand removes or keeps an area of similar colour, and the brush paints parts away or back. Hold Alt to do the opposite, and Undo steps back.
- Soften edge and Tighten edge tidy the cut-out's edge, and Show removed parts as checks makes it easy to see what's gone.
- The cut-out is see-through on the page, in exports and in the print PDF, turns with Flip and rotate, and a drop shadow takes its shape.

### Improved
- Copy effects leaves a removed background behind, because each photo needs its own.

## 0.1.18 · 25 Nov 2026
Save, open, export and bring in pictures from where you were last time.

### New
- Endpaper remembers the last folder you saved a design to, and File, Open starts there too, even after closing and opening Endpaper again.
- It remembers where you last brought in a picture, separately, so the picture picker opens there without changing where designs are saved.
- It remembers where you last exported, separately again, for print PDFs and pictures.

### Improved
- Save as starts in the folder of the design that's open.

## 0.1.17 · 25 Nov 2026
Your story keeps its empty lines, and whether they start a new page.

### New
- Format story has the Empty lines choice too, set to the one you placed the story with. Switch between Leave a gap and Start a new page at any time.

### Fixed

## 0.1.16 · 24 Nov 2026
Change your story's words and look in one place, and make the right panel as wide as you like.

### New
- Format story has your text in it: change the words there and the pages update as you type, with the look settings underneath. A line with just --- starts a new page.
- Drag the left edge of the right-hand panel to make it wider or narrower. Everything in it stretches to fit, the pages fit the space that's left, and Endpaper remembers the width. Double-click the edge to put it back.

## 0.1.15 · 24 Nov 2026
Paste your story and set how it looks, with the pages changing as you go.

### New
- Place text opens beside the pages, and your text appears on them as you paste or type. Nothing is kept until you press Done; Cancel puts everything back.
- Set the look while you place it: text style, font, weight, size, leading, letter spacing, space after paragraphs, indent, colour and alignment. The pages reflow with every change.
- Add pages if it needs more (Place text) adds pages after your range as the text grows, and takes them away again if it shrinks.
- Format story (Type, Format story…, or right-click linked text) changes how placed text looks later. If it no longer fits, add the pages it needs, or remove empty ones at the end.
- Save as new style… keeps the look as a text style, and Update changes the style you picked.

### Improved
- Changing the font, size or colour of linked text in Properties changes all of it, from any of its boxes.
- Placing text is one step to undo, including any pages it added.

## 0.1.14 · 24 Nov 2026
Paste your whole story and it flows across the pages.

### New
- Place text (Insert, Place text…) takes a long text you paste and puts it in a text box inside the margins of each page, from one page to another, with the text flowing from page to page.
- A line with just --- starts a new page. Empty lines can start a new page too, or leave a gap on the same page (Place text, Empty lines). Endpaper remembers your choice.
- Place text shows how many pages the text needs as you paste. If it needs more than you chose, add the pages it needs or stop at your last page.
- Pick the text style the text uses, and undo the whole thing in one go.

### Improved
- Typing in the Story window updates the thumbnail of every page the text runs through.

## 0.1.13 · 24 Nov 2026
Lay paper, canvas, grain and your own textures over your photos.

### New
- Texture, a new photo effect (Edit photo, Add, Detail and look), lays a texture over the photo: tiled at a size in mm, or stretched over it.
- 11 ready-made textures, drawn sharp at any size: fine paper, watercolour paper, canvas, linen, kraft card, recycled specks, crumpled paper, concrete, grunge, film dust and wood grain. Shuffle gives a fresh variation.
- Add your own textures (JPG, PNG or WebP) to a library you can use in every design. A design keeps a copy of any texture it uses, so it opens anywhere.
- Every texture is tagged Tiles or Doesn't tile. Endpaper checks your own when you add them; right-click one to change its tag, rename it or remove it from your library.
- A texture that doesn't tile is mirrored when tiled, so no hard joins show.
- Settings for angle, position, blend (Multiply, Overlay, Soft light, Screen, Normal), contrast, brightness, colour (its own, grey or a tint) and invert.
- Check warns when one of your own textures would print soft at the size you've set.

## 0.1.11 · 24 Nov 2026
Layer effects on your photos, like a photo editor.

### New
- Edit photo (Object, Edit photo, or Ctrl+Shift+E) opens a photo large, with its effects listed beside it like layers: add, hide, reorder by dragging, set the strength, and hold Before / After to compare.
- 24 effects: brightness and contrast, exposure, shadows and highlights, levels, curves, saturation and vibrance, warmth and tint, hue shift, black and white, duotone, colour overlay, invert, sharpen, blur, noise reduction, vignette, grain, halftone, threshold, posterise, flip and rotate, fade edges, blend modes and drop shadow.
- A photo's Properties lists its effects, to switch each one on or off without opening the window. Edit photo… is there and in the right-click menu too.
- Effects on a master's photo carry into a page's own copy (Ctrl+Shift+click), ready to change on that page. Effects you leave alone there still follow the master.
- Effects print as they look: they're in the PDF and PNG or JPG export, with fades, shadows and blend modes kept.

## 0.1.0 · 16 Nov 2026
Endpaper makes print-ready designs and books in CMYK.

### New
- New design and New book start from paper, card, poster and book sizes, or any size you type in mm, cm or px.
- Text boxes, photo frames, rectangles, ellipses and lines can be dragged, resized and rotated, and snap to edges, margins and each other (hold Ctrl to stop snapping).
- Layers list everything on the page, top first: drag to reorder, and hide or lock anything.
- Bleed, trim and the safe area show on every page, and photos and backgrounds can run into the bleed.
- Colours are CMYK, shown as they'll print on the printing profile you choose (Settings, Colour).
- Swatches and text styles: change one and everything using it changes.
- Books have facing pages, masters with page numbers ({page}), and a cover with the spine worked out from the page count and paper.
- Text can continue from one box into another, and the Story window shows all of it.
- Photos show their resolution as you resize them, and turn yellow or red when they'd print soft.
- Check lists what a printer would flag: low-resolution photos, text that doesn't fit, things that stop short of the bleed, text outside the safe area, rich black small text and too much ink.
- Export makes PDF/X-4 files with CMYK photos, embedded fonts, bleed and crop marks, or PNG and JPG at any resolution.
- Book export makes the inside pages and the cover as two PDFs, with or without bleed on the spine edge.
- The brand kit keeps your colours, text styles and logos ready for every design.
- Save any design as a template, and start new work from it on the start screen. Deleted templates stay in the bin for 30 days.
- Unsaved work is kept safe every 20 seconds, and offered back if Endpaper closes unexpectedly.
- Choose how Endpaper opens: in a window, maximised, full screen, or as you left it (Settings, General).
